# MGTECHS LIMITED - REGISTRATION CHECKLIST

## 🏢 1. Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) Registration Documents

### Essential Company Documents

| **Document** | **Description** | **Status** |
|--------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Certificate of Incorporation** | Proof of legal existence of your company. Issued by CAC upon completion  | ☐ To be obtained |
| **Memorandum and Articles of Association (MEMART)** | Defines your business purpose, objectives, and internal rules. Must be signed by all promoters  | ☐ To be prepared |
| **Form CAC 1.1** | Application for Registration containing company details, registered office address, share capital, and directors' particulars  | ☐ To be completed |
| **Form CAC 2.3 / Form C07** | Particulars of Directors - lists all company directors  | ☐ To be completed |
| **Form CAC 2.0 / Form C02** | Particulars of Shareholders - shows ownership structure  | ☐ To be completed |
| **Statement of Share Capital** | Declares the company's authorised and issued share capital  | ☐ To be prepared |
| **Statutory Declaration of Compliance** | Signed by a legal practitioner confirming all CAMA requirements have been met  | ☐ To be obtained |

### Identity & Verification Requirements

| **Document** | **Description** | **Status** |
|--------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Valid National ID** | National ID, International Passport, or Driver's License for all directors  | ☐ To be provided |
| **Bank Verification Number (BVN)** | For all signatories, directors, and beneficial owners  | ☐ To be provided |
| **Passport Photographs** | Recent passport photos for all directors and shareholders  | ☐ To be provided |
| **Proof of Address** | For signatories, directors, and company's registered address  | ☐ To be provided |
| **Residence Permit** | Required for non-Nigerian directors/shareholders  | ☐ N/A |

### Additional Requirements

| **Document** | **Description** | **Status** |
|--------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Company Name Availability Check** | At least two proposed names checked on CAC portal. Approved names reserved for 60 days (N500 fee)  | ☐ To be completed |
| **Registered Office Address** | Must be a physical address in Nigeria  | ☐ To be provided |
| **Company Email & Phone** | Valid contact details for CAC communication  | ☐ To be provided |
| **Board Resolution** | Authorizes account opening and appoints signatories (for banking)  | ☐ To be prepared |

## 📋 5. Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Step 1: Name Availability Search & Reservation
- Visit CAC portal: https://www.cac.gov.ng/
- Check availability of proposed company names
- Reserve approved name (60-day reservation) 

### Step 2: Prepare Incorporation Documents
- Prepare Memorandum & Articles of Association (MEMART) 
- Complete Form CAC 1.1
- Gather all director/shareholder identification documents
- Determine share capital and share distribution

### Step 3: Submit Online Application
- Log into CAC company registration portal
- Fill in all required fields
- Upload all required documents 

### Step 4: Pay Filing Fees
- Pay applicable fees online
- Pay stamp duty on share capital

### Step 5: CAC Review & Approval
- CAC reviews the submitted documents
- Upon approval, Certificate of Incorporation is issued
- CAC issues:
  - Certificate of Incorporation
  - Certified True Copies of MEMART
  - Status Report 

### Step 6: Post-Registration
- Register for TIN with FIRS 
- Register with NIPC (if foreign shareholders) 
- Obtain industry-specific licenses (if applicable)
- Open a corporate bank account
